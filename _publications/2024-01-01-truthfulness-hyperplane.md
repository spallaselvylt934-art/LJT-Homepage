On the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane Inside LLMs

I am the first author of this paper, which was published at EMNLP 2024. It studies the universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models (LLMs). Code repository: Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane.

Authors: Junteng Liu, Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He.
