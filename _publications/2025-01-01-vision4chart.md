On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding

I am the first author of this paper, which was published on arXiv in 2025. It studies the perception bottleneck of vision-language models (VLMs) for chart understanding. Code repository: Vision4Chart.

Authors: Junteng Liu, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He.
